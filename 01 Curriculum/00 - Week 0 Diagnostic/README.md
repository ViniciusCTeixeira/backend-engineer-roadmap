# Week 0 — Diagnostic

Week 0 establishes where the learner should enter the roadmap in depth and review frequency. It is **not** an elimination test.

## What it measures

The diagnostic samples backend fundamentals, PHP, databases, caching, Git, HTTP, testing, Linux, Docker, cloud, architecture, algorithms, technical English, coding-agent supervision, and introductory AI-engineering concepts.

## Rules

- Follow the declared `SOLO`, `HYBRID`, or `AI-ASSISTED` mode.
- Do not use an agent during `SOLO` tasks.
- Preserve your original answers before reviewing feedback.
- Record results only in your private `.study/` repository.
- Do not publish employer code, interview NDA material, secrets, customer data, or personal information.
- Low scores change the plan; they do not block participation.

## Duration

The blueprint targets six required days, each below 2.5 hours, with an optional seventh setup/review day.

## AI usage

The diagnostic intentionally includes both unaided engineering work and a coding-agent exercise. Strong agent-assisted performance does not automatically imply underlying conceptual mastery.

## After Week 0

Your private study state should contain a baseline per skill and an initial adaptation for the first four weeks. The public curriculum remains unchanged.

See `docs/design/week-0-diagnostic-design.md` for the approved blueprint.
