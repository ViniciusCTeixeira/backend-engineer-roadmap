# Curriculum Agent Rules

Scope: everything under `01 Curriculum/`.

Follow the root `AGENTS.md` first.

## Public curriculum governance

You may fix objective low-risk issues autonomously.

Create a public curriculum proposal before:
- adding/removing a topic;
- moving a topic between weeks/phases;
- changing prerequisites;
- changing a technology depth class;
- materially increasing/decreasing workload;
- changing a phase gate.

One learner's weakness or one vacancy is never sufficient evidence for a public curriculum edit.

## Sequence integrity

Do not introduce a practical requirement before its prerequisite concept is taught.

Preserve:
- four phase gates at Weeks 13, 26, 39, 52 unless a human-approved spec changes them;
- the seven transversal tracks;
- the 12-hour default weekly target;
- the distinction between core and supporting/exposure content.

## Personal adaptation

Never edit public curriculum files merely to mark a learner complete or weak. Learner-specific changes belong in `.study/`.
