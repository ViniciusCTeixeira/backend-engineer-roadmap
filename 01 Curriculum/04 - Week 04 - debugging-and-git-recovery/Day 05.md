## Activities

### Xdebug reading/setup — 25 min — `SOLO`

Review step-debugging activation and configure a safe local session.

### Hypothesis-first debug — 25 min — `SOLO`

Before a breakpoint, write the leading hypothesis and expected evidence.

### Step-debugging lab — 20 min — `SOLO`

Use Xdebug to inspect the selected Project A/synthetic bug and update the evidence log.

### Agent comparison — 15 min — `AI-ASSISTED`

Ask for alternative hypotheses only after your evidence exists; test plausible alternatives.

### Daily micro-assessment — 15 min — `SOLO`

Complete the Xdebug/debugging assessment.

### Decision note — 5 min — `HYBRID`

Record whether evidence confirmed/disproved your hypothesis.
