# Year 2 — Consolidation and International Readiness

Year 2 begins when the learner enters a meaningfully stronger backend role, not automatically on January 1 or exactly after Week 52.

If the stronger role is obtained early during Year 1, the Year 2 professional-development clock may begin earlier while the remaining roadmap study is reduced or resequenced.

## Purpose

Year 1 builds capability and obtains stronger engineering exposure.

Year 2 turns that exposure into demonstrated senior-level impact and international/relocation readiness.

## Default progression

### Months 0–3 — Adaptation and reliability

- understand the production system and domain;
- become reliable in delivery/debugging;
- learn operational expectations;
- build trust through consistent execution;
- identify high-leverage technical gaps privately.

Typical study target may reduce to approximately 6–8 hours/week when work itself provides strong engineering evidence.

### Months 3–6 — Ownership and measurable impact

- own meaningful backend work end-to-end;
- improve reliability/performance/testability;
- write useful design/operational documentation;
- participate more deeply in reviews;
- capture measurable impact for future interviews.

### Months 6–9 — Architecture and international exposure

- contribute to design decisions;
- handle ambiguous backend problems;
- improve technical English in real professional contexts;
- seek cross-team/international communication where available;
- mentor/review at an appropriate level.

### Months 9–12 — US/international campaign preparation

- close remaining interview gaps;
- update evidence-based résumé/portfolio;
- research target companies and relocation routes;
- practice English system-design/behavioral interviews;
- evaluate internal-transfer possibilities;
- begin an active relocation/sponsorship campaign when readiness criteria are met.

## Not a hard timer

The approximate 12-month consolidation period is a planning model, not a guarantee or waiting requirement.

The campaign may start earlier or later based on demonstrated readiness and opportunity.

## Evidence dimensions

Track privately:

- delivery reliability;
- scope/ownership;
- architecture/design;
- debugging/incident handling;
- data/performance;
- cloud/production;
- communication;
- professional English;
- mentoring/review;
- cross-team/international exposure;
- interview readiness;
- relocation/sponsorship research.

## Public/private boundary

Do not publish confidential employer projects, metrics, incidents, architecture, interview feedback, customer information, compensation, or immigration-case details.

Public portfolio updates must be generalized and employer-safe.
