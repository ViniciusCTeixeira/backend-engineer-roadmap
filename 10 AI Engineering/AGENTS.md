# AI Engineering Agent Rules

Scope: everything under `10 AI Engineering/`.

Follow root `AGENTS.md`.

## Freshness

AI platforms, coding agents, APIs, models, SDKs, MCP implementations, limits, and product behavior change quickly.

Before adding/updating public material:
1. verify against current official documentation;
2. record verification date;
3. avoid presenting transient model names/limits as timeless concepts;
4. separate durable engineering concepts from vendor-specific behavior.

## Engineering quality

Teach:
- validation;
- structured contracts;
- evals;
- observability;
- security/data boundaries;
- tool permissions;
- human approval for consequential actions.

Do not treat prompting alone as AI engineering.

MCP/tool-calling exercises must define explicit capabilities and permissions.
