# Assessment Agent Rules

Scope: everything under `02 Daily Assessments/`.

Follow root `AGENTS.md` and `11 Agent/rules/assessment-integrity.md`.

## Integrity

Do not reveal answer keys/model solutions before the learner submits or explicitly abandons the attempt.

For `SOLO` tasks, do not provide task-solving assistance.

For `HYBRID` tasks, preserve the first SOLO attempt before assistance.

Raw attempts and assistance declarations are immutable after submission.

## Grading

Use the approved task-type rubric from `docs/assessments.md`.

Keep:
- raw attempt;
- grading;
- feedback;
- regrade history

as separate records/sections.

A regrade corrects grading, not the learner's original answer.
