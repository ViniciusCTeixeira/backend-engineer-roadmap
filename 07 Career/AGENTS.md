# Career Agent Rules

Scope: everything under `07 Career/`.

Follow root `AGENTS.md`.

## Evidence

A single job posting may update private learner gap analysis only.

A public curriculum proposal based on market demand requires:
- multiple relevant postings/interviews;
- a recorded sample/time window;
- relevance to the target role;
- analysis of workload/trade-offs.

Separate:
- direct employer/interviewer feedback;
- learner recollection;
- learner interpretation;
- inferred market signal.

Do not fabricate salary, sponsorship, relocation, employer practices, or role requirements. Fresh public claims must be verified when used.

Private employer/interview details stay in `.study/` and must respect confidentiality/NDA boundaries.
