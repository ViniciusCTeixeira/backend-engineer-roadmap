# Interview Retrospective Method

Store real retrospectives privately under `.study/career/interviews/` using `90 Templates/interview-retrospective.md`.

The goal is to turn an interview into evidence without overreacting to one outcome.

## 1. Capture quickly

Preserve the original recollection before interpretation changes it.

Record date, company identity privately, round, format, questions/tasks remembered, learner response, direct feedback actually received, and outcome when known.

Respect NDA/confidentiality boundaries. Do not reconstruct proprietary interview banks for public distribution.

## 2. Separate evidence layers

### Layer A — factual recollection
What was asked or done.

### Layer B — learner response
What you answered, coded, designed, or explained.

### Layer C — direct external feedback
Only statements actually made by the interviewer or recruiter.

### Layer D — interpretation
Your hypothesis about what went well or poorly.

Interpretation must not be presented as interviewer feedback.

## 3. Classify gap signals

Possible categories:

- technical knowledge;
- reasoning/trade-offs;
- debugging;
- SQL/data;
- coding/algorithms;
- system design;
- cloud/production;
- communication;
- technical English;
- behavioral/impact;
- time management;
- requirement misunderstanding;
- validation/testing;
- AI/tool dependence.

## 4. Assign evidence confidence

```text
high
  direct interviewer feedback or clearly failed task

medium
  strong learner recollection with concrete evidence

low
  learner speculation without direct evidence
```

Low-confidence interpretation should not trigger large study-plan changes.

## 5. Convert evidence into remediation

A supported gap may create D+1, D+7, D+30 reviews, targeted recovery, a mock interview, project evidence improvement, or English/communication practice.

Use the existing adaptive-learning rules rather than inventing a separate career remediation algorithm.

## 6. Preserve historical integrity

Never rewrite original interview notes, remembered questions/tasks, outcome, or direct feedback.

Append later interpretation, correction, or evidence separately.

## 7. Public curriculum rule

One interview failure changes private preparation only.

A possible public curriculum problem requires generalized recurring evidence from multiple independent interviews/jobs/learners or other strong sources.

## 8. Successful interviews are evidence too

Record what was demonstrated strongly: SQL reasoning, system design, English fluency, production ownership, coding performance, or behavioral impact.

Strong repeated evidence may reduce redundant private repetition while mandatory phase-gate coverage remains.

## 9. Funnel metrics

Private career metrics may aggregate:

```text
jobs analyzed
applications
recruiter screens
technical interviews
system-design interviews
take-homes
final rounds
offers
declines
```

Use funnel metrics to diagnose process problems, not as a measure of personal worth.
