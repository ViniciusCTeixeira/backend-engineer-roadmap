## Resource use rule

A resource is not a task.

Generated daily study plans must specify:
- what to read/watch/use;
- what to practice;
- what evidence to produce;
- how mastery will be checked.

## Fast-moving content

AI/cloud/platform references are re-verified at task-generation/use time. The catalog's `last_verified` date is evidence of the last audit, not a guarantee that vendor behavior will never change.
